## Editorial & Classification Guardrails

### 1. Five-Category Place Classification Taxonomy
Every sub-county place record in this document is explicitly assigned one canonical classification from the required 5-tier taxonomy:

| Classification Slug | Definition & Usage Rule |
|---|---|
| `incorporated-municipality` | Legally incorporated Alabama municipal corporation (classified under *Code of Alabama* § 11-40-6 as a **City** if population $\ge$ 2,000, or a **Town** if population $<$ 2,000). Has elected mayor/council and municipal boundary lines. |
| `official-neighborhood` | Formally mapped district or neighborhood boundary recognized by the City of Mobile, Mobile Historic Development Commission (MHDC Local Architectural Review Board district), National Register of Historic Places (NRHP), or official Downtown Business Improvement District. |
| `recognized-community` | Historically, culturally, or geographically distinct named settlement or community (such as Africatown/Plateau, Spring Hill, Crichton, Toulminville, The Peninsula of Mobile, Dog River, or named rural/coastal settlements) that has genuine community identity and planning recognition even if it is not an incorporated town or a single MHDC architectural review polygon. |
| `local-area-name` | Broad, commonly used everyday regional or corridor name (such as **West Mobile**, **Midtown Mobile**, or **Cottage Hill**) that spans multiple subdivisions, historic districts, or city/unincorporated boundary lines without a single official municipal polygon. Never claim hard street-by-street borders for a `local-area-name`. |
| `unincorporated-area` | Area outside any incorporated municipal limits governed directly by the County Commission (including U.S. Census-Designated Places / CDPs like Theodore, Tillmans Corner, Grand Bay, and Point Clear, or split/rural areas). |

### 2. Non-Negotiable Content Accuracy Rules
1. **Never extrapolate City of Mobile trash or drop-off rules to other jurisdictions:** City of Mobile biweekly curbside bulk ("trash") rules and the 3 City of Mobile Recycling Drop-Off Centers apply **strictly inside City of Mobile corporate limits**. Residents of Semmes, Saraland, Prichard, Theodore, Tillman's Corner, and all of Baldwin County are governed by completely different municipal or private-subscription systems.
2. **Never invent municipal bulk rules:** Where a smaller town's bulk/appliance rules are handled by private contract, utility board, or periodic schedule subject to change, content must state verified general structure only and advise residents to **"verify current municipal bulk rules with City/Town Hall or your contracted hauler."**
3. **Never invent neighborhood polygons:** Only cite boundaries where officially defined by NRHP nominations, MHDC district maps, water bodies, or named master plans (e.g., *The Peninsula of Mobile Corridor Master Plan*). For `local-area-name` entries, describe major corridors rather than artificial bounding boxes.
4. **USPS Postal City $\ne$ Municipal Incorporation:** Many unincorporated areas carry the postal city name of a nearby incorporated city (e.g., Belforest uses `Daphne, AL 36526`; Battles Wharf and Barnwell use `Fairhope, AL 36532`; Fort Morgan uses `Gulf Shores, AL 36542`; parts of Tillman's Corner and West Mobile use `Mobile, AL 36619` / `36695`). Always distinguish mailing address from municipal jurisdiction.

### 1.1 Mobile County, AL

- **Official Name:** Mobile County, Alabama
- **County Seat:** City of Mobile (Alabama's only Class 2 municipality under AL Code § 11-40-12)
- **Physical Geography & Boundaries:**
  - Situated in the southwestern corner of Alabama along the Gulf Coast.
  - **East:** Bounded by the Mobile River, the Mobile-Tensaw River Delta, and the western shore of Mobile Bay.
  - **South:** Bounded by Mississippi Sound, Dauphin Island (Gulf barrier island), and the Gulf of Mexico.
  - **West:** Borders Jackson, George, and Greene counties, Mississippi.
  - **North:** Borders Washington County, Alabama.
  - **Topography & Watersheds:** Transitions from rolling pine-forested uplands in the north (Citronelle and Mount Vernon) to flat Gulf Coastal Plain suburbs in West/South Mobile, urban tidal watersheds (Three Mile Creek, Chickasabogue Creek, Dog River, Fowl River, Deer River / Theodore Industrial Canal), working seafood bayous (Bayou La Batre, Coden), and coastal marshlands/barrier islands.
- **Major Transportation Corridors:**
  - **I-10:** Primary east-west transcontinental interstate crossing southern Mobile County (Grand Bay, Theodore, Tillman's Corner, South/Downtown Mobile) into Baldwin County via the George Wallace Tunnel and I-10 Bayway.
  - **I-65:** Primary north-south interstate bisecting Mobile, Prichard/Chickasaw, Saraland, Satsuma, and Creola toward Montgomery.
  - **I-165:** Urban spur linking I-65 in Prichard/Chickasaw southward along the Prichard/Africatown/Downtown Mobile industrial corridor to US-90/98 (Water Street).
  - **US-90:** Historic east-west coastal artery entering from Mississippi through Grand Bay, Irvington, Theodore, and Tillman's Corner (Government Blvd / Hwy 90), continuing as Government Street into Downtown Mobile and crossing Mobile Bay via Battleship Parkway ("The Causeway").
  - **US-98:** Enters from Mississippi in northwest Mobile County as **Moffett Road** through Wilmer and **Semmes**, continues southeast as **Spring Hill Avenue** into Midtown/Downtown Mobile, and crosses the Mobile River via the **Bankhead Tunnel** onto the Causeway.
  - **US-43:** Major north-south industrial corridor running north from Prichard/Chickasaw (Telegraph Rd / Craft Hwy) through **Saraland**, **Satsuma**, **Creola**, Axis, Bucks, and **Mount Vernon**.
  - **US-45:** Northwest corridor (**St. Stephens Road**) running from Mobile through Toulminville, **Prichard**, **Eight Mile**, Chunchula, and **Citronelle** into Mississippi.
  - **State & Local Arteries:** **AL-163** (Dauphin Island Pkwy along the Mobile Peninsula and across Dog River Bridge), **AL-193** (southern Dauphin Island Pkwy / Gordon Persons Bridge onto Dauphin Island), **AL-188** (coastal loop serving Bayou La Batre, Coden, and AL-193), **AL-158** (Industrial Pkwy linking Semmes/US-98 to I-65/Saraland), **Airport Blvd**, **Cottage Hill Rd**, and **Schillinger Rd**.
- **Solid Waste, Landfills & Recycling Overview:**
  - **Mobile County Commission Programs (`mobilecountyal.gov`):**
    - **Mobile County Recycling Center:** `7450 Hitt Road, Mobile, AL 36695` — County-owned residential drop-off operated by Goodwill Gulf Coast (requires sorting by material type; accepts paper, cardboard, plastics #1–7, glass bottles/jars, metal cans, clothing, and select e-waste via Ransom partnership; no CRT TVs or construction debris).
    - **Operation Clean Sweep:** Community cleanup drop-off events organized via Mobile County Environmental Enforcement (`251-574-STOP`) for Mobile County residents with proof of residency (no businesses, no dump trailers, 1 load per household; accepts appliances, flat-screen TVs/monitors, household bulk, yard debris, scrap metal, and up to 10 tires; **excludes CRT tube TVs/monitors**, paint, and household garbage).
  - **Unincorporated Mobile County Governance:** Mobile County **does not** provide countywide curbside residential garbage or curbside bulk trash collection. Residents in unincorporated communities (**Theodore, Tillman's Corner, Grand Bay, Irvington, unincorporated Eight Mile, Unincorporated West Mobile**) must contract individually with private waste haulers (e.g., Goodwin Sanitation, H&M Sanitation, American Waste Solutions, Waste Pro, Waste Management, Republic Services), which generally cover standard 95/96-gallon bagged household garbage carts and charge extra or exclude large cleanouts, appliances, e-waste, and construction debris.
  - **City of Mobile Municipal Rules (`cityofmobile.gov` — inside Mobile city limits ONLY):**
    - **Curbside Bulk/Yard ("Trash"):** Every-other-week pickup capped at **2 cubic yards** (`3' x 3' x 6'`); prohibits mixing electronics, TVs, Freon-bearing appliances (refrigerators, freezers, ACs), tires, glass, paint/chemicals, contractor debris, or eviction cleanout piles (fines up to $532 for placing piles >48 hours early or illegal dumping).
    - **City Recycling Centers (3 sites, City of Mobile residents ONLY):** `4851 Museum Dr` (Langan Park), `308 Pinehill Dr`, and `1750 Dauphin Island Pkwy`. Single-stream (glass discontinued April 2024); blue Ransom Solutions bins accept small residential corded electronics, **strictly NO TVs of any kind**.
    - **Household Hazardous Waste (HHW):** Periodic City HHW collection events for Mobile residents only (`cityofmobile.gov/hhw/`; excludes appliances and electronics).
  - **Landfills Serving Mobile County:**
    - **Chastang Sanitary Landfill:** `17045 Highway 43, Mount Vernon, AL 36560` — Primary regional Municipal Solid Waste (MSW) & demolition landfill.
    - **C&D / Trash Landfills:** **Dirt, Inc.** (`8081 Cottage Hill Rd, Mobile`), **H & S Land, Inc.** (`8700 Howells Ferry Rd, Semmes`), and **Waste Pro Lott Road Landfill** (`5400 Dossett Rd, Prichard/Eight Mile, AL 36613`).

### 1.2 Baldwin County, AL

- **Official Name:** Baldwin County, Alabama
- **County Seat:** City of Bay Minette
- **Physical Geography & Sub-Regions:**
  - Geographically the **largest county in Alabama** (~1,590 sq mi land, ~2,027 sq mi total area — larger than the state of Rhode Island) and one of the fastest-growing counties in the Southeast.
  - **West:** Bounded by Mobile Bay, the Blakeley/Apalachee/Tensaw Rivers, and the Mobile-Tensaw River Delta.
  - **East:** Bounded by the Perdido River and Perdido Bay along the Florida state line (Escambia County, FL / Pensacola metro).
  - **South:** Bounded by the Gulf of Mexico across Pleasure Island and the 22-mile Fort Morgan Peninsula.
  - **North:** Bounded by the Alabama River / Little River bordering Monroe, Clarke, and Escambia counties, AL.
  - **Four Distinct Sub-Regions (Standard Baldwin County Regional Framework):**
    1. **Eastern Shore:** **Spanish Fort, Daphne, Montrose, Fairhope, Battles Wharf, Point Clear, Barnwell, Belforest.** Defined by scenic red-clay bluffs overlooking Mobile Bay, mature live-oak residential avenues, commuter access to Mobile via the I-10 Bayway and US-90/98 Causeway, and rapid suburban subdivision growth inland along **AL-181** and **CR-64**.
    2. **North Baldwin:** **Bay Minette (County Seat), Stockton, Stapleton, Perdido, Little River.** Defined by rolling pine timberlands, historic crossroads, the vast Mobile-Tensaw River Delta ecosystem, industrial megasites, and the **I-65**, **US-31**, **AL-59**, and **AL-225** corridors.
    3. **Central Baldwin:** **Loxley, Robertsdale, Silverhill, Summerdale, Elberta, Magnolia Springs, Perdido Beach, Historic Marlow, Fish River, Lillian.** The agricultural, logistics, and residential heartland stretching from I-10 south toward Foley and east to Perdido Bay. Anchored by **AL-59**, **US-90**, **US-98**, **AL-104**, the **Baldwin Beach Express**, **Fish River** (draining into Weeks Bay), **Magnolia River**, and **Perdido Bay**. *(Note: Elberta, Perdido Beach, and Lillian form the eastern US-98 / Perdido Bay wing of Central/South-East Baldwin; Magnolia Springs bridges Central and South Baldwin).*
    4. **South Baldwin / Pleasure Island:** **Foley, Gulf Shores, Orange Beach, Bon Secour, Fort Morgan** (plus Ono Island). Defined by high-volume coastal tourism, vacation rentals/condominiums, commercial retail centers along **AL-59** and the **Foley Beach Express**, the **Gulf Intracoastal Waterway (GIWW)**, **Bon Secour River**, **Wolf Bay**, **Little Lagoon**, **Perdido Pass**, **AL-182 (Perdido Beach Blvd)**, **AL-180 (Fort Morgan Rd)**, and **AL-161**. *(Note: Foley sits just north of Pleasure Island and functions as the commercial gateway bridging Central and South Baldwin).*
- **Solid Waste, Landfills & Recycling Overview — Solid Waste Disposal Authority of Baldwin County (SWDA):**
  - **Authority:** Countywide disposal facilities, recycling processing, and unincorporated garbage collection are operated by the **Solid Waste Disposal Authority of Baldwin County (SWDA)** (`baldwincountyswda.org`, phone `251-972-6878`).
  - **Primary Landfills, MRF & Transfer Facilities:**
    - **Magnolia Sanitary Landfill (MSW):** `15480 Terra Bella Drive, Summerdale, AL 36580` — Primary countywide municipal solid waste landfill for household garbage, bulky waste, and commercial haulers. Also houses Baldwin County's **62,000-sq-ft Materials Recovery Facility (MRF)** (processes single-stream recycling across the county) and an on-site Renewable Natural Gas (RNG) capture plant.
    - **MacBride Landfill (C&D):** `26941 McBride Road, Loxley, AL 36551` — Construction & demolition debris landfill serving central/northern/western Baldwin County.
    - **Eastfork Landfill (C&D):** `29751 Eastfork Landfill Road, Elberta, AL 36530` — Construction & demolition debris landfill serving eastern/southern Baldwin County.
    - **Bay Minette Transfer Station:** `43205 Nicholsville Road, Bay Minette, AL 36507` — Waste transfer and drop-off hub serving North Baldwin.
  - **CHaRM (Center for Hard-to-Recycle Materials) Drop-Off Locations:**
    - Accept residential specialty items such as electronics, household hazardous waste (HHW), paint, batteries, and select hard-to-recycle materials (schedules and item fees apply — verify current SWDA fee schedule before quoting):
      1. **Magnolia CHaRM:** `15101 Terra Bella Drive, Summerdale, AL 36580`
      2. **Fairhope CHaRM:** `20609 Bishop Road, Fairhope, AL 36532`
      3. **Orange Beach CHaRM:** `22510 Russian Road, Gulf Shores / Orange Beach, AL 36542`
  - **Countywide Single-Stream Recycling Rules & Unmanned Drop-Offs:**
    - SWDA maintains unmanned drop-off trailers/sites across the county (including Bay Minette Transfer Station, Bay Minette Public Works, MacBride Landfill, Stockton, Stapleton VFD, Elsanor Community Center, Weeks Bay, Foley Courthouse, Perdido VFD, and Spanish Fort Kids Park).
    - **Strict MRF Contamination Rule:** Across Baldwin County's single-stream MRF network (including municipal carts feeding into the Magnolia MRF), **glass, plastic bags, and Styrofoam are prohibited** in standard single-stream recycling bins.
  - **CRITICAL GOVERNANCE DISTINCTION (Unincorporated vs. Incorporated Baldwin Municipalities):**
    - **SWDA Residential Curbside Service Area:** Covers **all unincorporated Baldwin County** (Belforest, Point Clear, Montrose, Battles Wharf, Barnwell, Marlow, Fish River, Bon Secour, Lillian, Fort Morgan, Stapleton, Stockton, etc.) **PLUS 4 participating municipalities** (**Bay Minette, Summerdale, Magnolia Springs, and Perdido Beach**). Active residential accounts receive weekly 95-gallon cart garbage pickup and **up to one free scheduled bulky/yard waste pickup per month** (excludes contractor C&D debris, commercial tree work, hazardous waste, and oversized piles; extra loads incur truckload fees).
    - **Independent Municipal Sanitation Cities/Towns (10):** **Daphne, Fairhope, Spanish Fort, Foley, Gulf Shores, Orange Beach, Robertsdale, Loxley, Elberta, and Silverhill** each run their own municipal Public Works sanitation divisions or private franchise contracts (e.g., Waste Pro in Spanish Fort; Republic Services for garbage + City Public Works for bulk/yard debris in Gulf Shores) with **completely different volume caps, frequencies, and restrictions**. Content must **never** apply SWDA monthly bulk rules to non-participating cities like Spanish Fort, Daphne, Fairhope, Foley, Gulf Shores, or Orange Beach.

  - Solid Waste Disposal Authority of Baldwin County (SWDA): https://baldwincountyswda.org/

### 4.1 Critical Jurisdictional Verification: Eight Mile, AL (`36613`)
- **Is Eight Mile incorporated?** **No** — Eight Mile has never been an independent incorporated municipality.
- **Does Eight Mile overlap Prichard city limits?** **YES — Split Jurisdiction.** Named for its location ~8 miles northwest of downtown Mobile along **US-45 (St. Stephens Road)** and **AL-217 (Lott Road)**, significant eastern and commercial portions of historic Eight Mile were **annexed by the City of Prichard** in the mid-20th century, while western and northern stretches along Lott Road / Spice Pond Road remain **unincorporated Mobile County**.
- **SEO & Service Rule for Eight Mile:** Both the Prichard-annexed portion and the unincorporated portion share **USPS ZIP code `36613`**. Neither portion is inside the City of Mobile (so City of Mobile curbside rules and recycling drop-offs do not apply). Properties inside Prichard city limits follow City of Prichard sanitation rules; properties outside Prichard limits are unincorporated Mobile County and require private waste haulers. Note also that Waste Pro's **Lott Road C&D Landfill** (`5400 Dossett Rd`) sits in the `36613` Eight Mile/Prichard corridor.
